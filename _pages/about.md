I am a final-year PhD student in the Data Science and AI Division of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Chalmers University of Technology. My research focuses on developing AI systems and methods to support scientific discovery, with a particular focus on the yeast Saccharomyces cerevisiae as a model organism. I work at the intersection of machine learning, knowledge representation, and knowledge graphs, developing methods that can combine structured scientific knowledge with data-driven learning. In particular, my research explores neurosymbolic approaches for incorporating domain knowledge and hierarchical structure into AI models. A central aim of this work is to use AI to generate and prioritise biological hypotheses, which can then be experimentally tested and verified. The overarching goal is to develop AI systems that can better exploit existing scientific knowledge while learning from data, enabling more accurate, interpretable, and scientifically useful predictions and accelerating the process of scientific discovery.

My PhD is supervised by [Ross D. King](https://www.chalmers.se/en/persons/rossk/).
